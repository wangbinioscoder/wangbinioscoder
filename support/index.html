Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start my first workout?

Open the Training tab, tap on any workout card to expand it, then tap "Start Training" to enter guided workout mode.

Can I customize my weekly training plan?

Yes! Go to the Profile tab and tap the edit button. You can set your fitness goal, available equipment, and preferred session duration. The app will adapt your weekly plan accordingly.

How do achievements work?

Achievements are unlocked automatically as you reach milestones such as completing your first workout, maintaining a 7-day streak, or training for a total of 10 hours. Check your progress in the Profile tab.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled for your device, your data will be included in your regular iCloud backups.

How do I enable or disable voice guidance?

Go to the Profile tab, tap the gear icon to open Settings, and toggle "Enable Voice Guidance". You can also select your preferred voice language there.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. FitTrainer works entirely offline. All features, including workout plans, tracking, and progress charts, are available without an internet connection.
