Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

FitTrainer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: FitTrainer stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal information with any server or third party.

Information We Collect

FitTrainer does not collect any personal information through external servers. All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). The data stored locally includes:

  • Your profile information (nickname, fitness goal, equipment preference, session duration)
  • Your training records and workout history
  • Your achievement progress
  • Your app settings (voice guidance, notifications, language preference)

Camera and Pose Recognition

FitTrainer may use your device's camera for real-time form feedback during workouts. All camera footage and pose recognition processing occurs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning frameworks. We do not record, upload, or share any video footage.

How We Use Your Information

The locally stored data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app:

  • Generating personalized weekly training plans
  • Tracking your workout progress and displaying statistics
  • Calculating and displaying achievements
  • Providing voice guidance during workouts
  • Sending local training reminder notifications (if enabled)

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share your data with any third parties. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud storage providers. Your data never leaves your device.

Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device passcodes and biometric authentication (if enabled). Since all data is stored locally, its security is equivalent to the security of your device itself.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can modify or delete your profile information at any time within the app
  • You can clear all app data by deleting the app from your device
  • You can disable notifications in the app's Settings or your device's system settings

Children's Privacy

FitTrainer does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store and does not contain any content inappropriate for children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
